# Reflection from Part A

Before this activity, I thought of AI mostly as a tool that answers questions. Writing Part A showed me that an AI agent is different: it can take a goal, decide on steps, and act on its own. That is useful, but it also means mistakes can have real consequences.

What stood out most is that the old ideas from Sommerville still apply. Small increments, regular reviews, testing, and version control are even more useful when an agent is making changes. The same goes for the idea that people make mistakes and systems should catch them, since AI can be wrong too.

The main thing I take from this is that humans stay accountable. Agents can propose and help, but people should review the work and approve the high-impact decisions.
